# Speech Content Sources

This file tracks reputable resources used to curate/adapt word, imitation-action, and song prompts in `jessy-speech-coach.jsx`.

Image attribution and licensing details are tracked separately in `speech-image-sources.md`.

## Clinical and AAC Sources

- ASHA (AAC in early intervention): https://www.asha.org/practice/early-intervention-provider-support/augmentative-and-alternative-communication-in-early-intervention/
- PrAACtical AAC (core vocabulary lists and samples): https://praacticalaac.org/praactical/aac-vocabulary-lists/
- Hanen Centre (imitation and parent-led interaction strategies): https://www.hanen.org/information-tips/imitate-your-child-to-spark-an-interaction
- Communication Matrix (early communication/joint attention foundations): https://www.communicationmatrix.org/
- UNICEF / WHO Care for Child Development: https://www.unicef.org/documents/care-child-development
- Sheffield Children's NHS core vocabulary examples: https://library.sheffieldchildrens.nhs.uk/core-vocabulary-stage-1-programme/

## Licensing / Attribution Notes

- The in-app entries are adapted short prompts for educational practice, not full copyrighted song lyrics.
- Classical English nursery rhymes are commonly public domain, but regional variants may differ.
- Telugu rhyme material can vary by publisher and modern adaptations; verify per-item rights before commercial redistribution.
- For strict compliance in a published app, replace adapted lines with:
  - public-domain-only lines with explicit source edition metadata, or
  - original in-house prompts inspired by therapy techniques.
